First Paper Submitted

February 4, 2026 General 2 min read

I have just submitted my first paper. Based on my master thesis, we — myself, Leon Lettermann (my supervisor), and Ulrich Schwarz — have written a paper about clathrin-mediated endocytosis, and have submitted it to Science Advances.

What to Expect

I plan to write about a variety of topics, but here are some themes you can expect to see:

  • Physics and Science: Insights from my research, interesting papers I've read, and explanations of complex concepts in accessible ways.
  • Research Journey: Updates on my master's thesis work, challenges I face, and what I'm learning along the way.
  • Books and Reading: Thoughts on books I'm reading, from fantasy epics like Wheel of Time to scientific literature.
  • Life as a Student: Experiences from university life, study tips, and reflections on the academic journey.
  • Technology and Computing: Programming projects, tools I'm using, and the intersection of computation and physics.

Why Start Now?

I've always enjoyed writing and explaining complex ideas. Over the years, I've realized that writing helps me think more clearly and deeply about topics. It forces me to organize my thoughts and consider different perspectives. Plus, if my experiences and insights can help or inspire even one person, that makes it worthwhile.

A Work in Progress

Like any good research project, this blog will evolve over time. I might experiment with different formats, topics, and styles. Some posts might be technical deep-dives, while others could be brief reflections or updates. I hope you'll join me on this journey and perhaps share your thoughts along the way.

Feel free to reach out if you have questions, suggestions, or just want to chat about any of the topics I cover. You can find my contact information on the main page.

Thanks for reading, and stay tuned for more posts!

— Johannes
