Education

I studied physics at Heidelberg University, with a growing focus on theoretical biophysics, nonlinear pattern formation, and simulation-based models of cellular processes. My bachelor thesis explored pattern formation in lipid membranes; my master thesis moved toward clathrin-mediated endocytosis and the mechanics of growing protein coats on membranes.

Current Research

I recently released a preprint based on my master thesis work in the Schwarz group. The project studies how clathrin coats grow, stiffen, and deform membranes during clathrin-mediated endocytosis. In September 2026, I will start my PhD at ISTA.
